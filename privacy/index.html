Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026. FlyAway is designed to minimize data handling and keep message filtering on your device.

Message filtering

Message sender numbers and message content are processed by the FlyAway Message Filter Extension on your device. FlyAway does not transmit, store on developer-operated servers, sell, or use this information for advertising or profiling.

Mock values entered in the rule test stay in memory and are not saved as test history.

Rules and local storage

Your ordered rules are stored locally on your device and shared only with the FlyAway extension through an Apple App Group container.

Optional iCloud sync

If you purchase and enable iCloud sync, your rule document is stored in the private CloudKit database associated with your iCloud account. Message content, sender numbers from received messages, and match history are not synchronized.

You can disable sync and delete the cloud rule document from the app. Rules already stored locally remain available until you delete them.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit processes the one-time iCloud sync purchase and restoration. FlyAway does not receive your payment-card details.

Analytics and third parties

FlyAway contains no advertising SDK, tracking SDK, developer analytics service, or developer-operated message classification service.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to flyaway@mebtte.com.
