Good to know

Does FlyAway read every message?

No. iOS decides which messages from unknown senders are offered to third-party filters. Known senders and senders you have repeatedly replied to may bypass filtering.

Are Simplified and Traditional Chinese converted?

No. Rules are matched exactly as you enter them. FlyAway never translates or performs language conversion.

Does Filter delete a message?

No. A matching message is classified into the system’s junk or filtered area. FlyAway does not delete it.
